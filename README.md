# Cafeteria-Management-System
Cafeteria Management System is solution built to modernize food service operations. It helps to avoid long queues by allowing users to browse menu, place online orders of their choice.
In this particular project we have used three modules
menu.py, customers.py and main.py their explanations are as follows:
menu.py : Contains the food items, prices, and functions to display the available menu.
customers.py : Handles customer data, storing names, or linking orders to specific buyers.
main.py : Its purpose is importing functions from menu.py and customers.py to get the user for their name and order choices.
After following these steps we will enter food of our choice, customer's name who is ordering and then view the final receipt.
